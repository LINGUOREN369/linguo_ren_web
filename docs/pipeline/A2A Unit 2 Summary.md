# A2A Unit 2 Summary — Quadratics

> **Course:** Accelerated Algebra 2 · **Days:** 6 (Sep 28 – Oct 9) · **Assessment:** Quiz 2, Day 6
> Published excerpt: the quiz analysis and source-file notes are left out.

---

## The unit in one line

Every quadratic can be solved — so build the full toolkit, learn to pick the cheapest tool from
the shape of the equation, and finally learn to predict how many solutions exist before solving
at all.

## The arc

The unit moves through three stages, and the day-to-day decisions follow from which stage you
are in:

1. **Days 1–3 — build the method.** Square roots, then completing the square (practised on its
   own on Day 2 with leading coefficient 1), then completing
   the square *in general* on Day 3, which produces the quadratic formula. Nothing is memorized
   that was not first derived.
2. **Day 3 (second half) — choose the method.** Five equations, five cues, each solved the cheap
   way. The takeaway is the list of cues, not the five answers.
3. **Days 4–5 — extend and consolidate.** Systems extend the same solving work to two equations
   at once, alongside more mixed method practice. Day 5 puts everything
   under one constraint and surfaces the extraneous-root trap the quiz turns on.

**The single most important block in the unit** is Day 3's derivation of the quadratic formula
from $ax^{2}+bx+c=0$. It is the only place the formula stops being a magic string, and it is
also what explains a "no real solution" answer: a negative under the radical. It gets 18 minutes and should never be
compressed.

## Day-by-day goals

| Day | Lesson | Goal — what a student can do by the end | New this day | What it sets up |
| --: | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A2A Day 1 - Solving Quadratics by Square Roots and Completing the Square | Solve any quadratic whose squared quantity can be isolated, keeping both roots; produce the constant that completes the square for $x^{2}+bx$ | Square-root method; the $\left(\frac{b}{2}\right)^{2}$ rule; "no real solution" when a square equals a negative | Everything — Days 2–5 all rest on completing the square |
| 2 | A2A Day 2 - Completing the Square Practice | Solve any $x^{2}+bx+c=0$ by completing the square, including odd $b$ and equations that must be rearranged first | Fluency only — no new idea; fractions from odd $b$; simplifying the radical in the answer | Day 3, where only the $a\neq 1$ bookkeeping is new |
| 3 | A2A Day 3 - Quadratic Formula and Choosing a Solving Method | Complete the square when $a\neq 1$, **derive** the quadratic formula, and pick the cheapest of five methods from an equation's structure | Factoring out $a$; equation vs. expression; the derivation; the five structural cues | Quiz problem 3 (each method once) |
| 4 | A2A Day 4 - Systems of Equations and Mixed Practice | Solve a system in which at least one equation is quadratic, by substitution or elimination, and choose a solving method without being told | Systems with a quadratic; elimination on squared terms; mixed method practice | Quiz problems 4 and 5 |
| 5 | A2A Day 5 - Quadratic Partner Problem Set | Apply each method once under constraint, and recognize when the only candidate root is extraneous | Extraneous roots in a rational equation that collapses to a restricted value | The quiz — this day is the rehearsal for it |
